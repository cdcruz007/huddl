By using Huddl, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

3 Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance)
Under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and Data Protection Act 2018, we process your personal data based on the following legal grounds: a) Contractual Necessity (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to perform our contract with you — account creation, Service features, payments, communications. Without this processing, we cannot provide the Service. b) Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): • Improving and developing the Service • Personalizing user experience • Fraud prevention and security • Marketing to existing users about similar services You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. c) Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): • Responding to law enforcement requests • Complying with court orders and legal processes • Meeting tax and accounting requirements d) Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): We obtain your explicit consent for: • Precise GPS location tracking • Marketing communications • Non-essential cookies and tracking • Voice message recording and microphone access (see Section 16) You may withdraw consent at any time via in-app settings or by emailing privacy@huddl.app. e) Vital Interests (Article 6(1)(d)): In rare cases — emergency situations or child safety concerns requiring immediate action. f) Special Categories: We do NOT intentionally collect special categories of personal data (health data, racial origin, political opinions, etc.).

6 Data Retention and Deletion
Active accounts: Data retained while your account is active. Account deletion: Most personal data deleted within 30 days; up to 90 days for account recovery/dispute resolution. Retained after deletion: • Financial transaction records — 7 years (tax law) • Legal/compliance records — as required by law • Anonymized/aggregated data — indefinitely • Fraud prevention records — indefinitely (where account was terminated for abuse) Voice messages: Retained for the life of the conversation or until account deletion. All voice data in Firebase Storage is permanently deleted on account deletion. Your right to erasure: Email privacy@huddl.app or use Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account.

16 🎙️ Voice Messages & Microphone Access
a) Overview: Huddl provides a voice message feature allowing you to record and send short audio messages within group chats and direct messages. This section explains specifically how we handle microphone access and voice message data. b) Microphone Permission: • We request access to your device microphone solely to enable voice message recording • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO (Android) and NSMicrophoneUsageDescription (iOS) permissions are used exclusively for this feature • Microphone access is active only during the brief period you are actively recording • We do NOT listen to, record, or access your microphone at any other time • You may deny or revoke microphone permission at any time in device settings — this disables voice message recording only c) Data Collected: • Audio recordings — temporary M4A/AAC files (mobile) or WebM (web) created during recording • Recording metadata — duration (seconds) and timestamp • Conversation context — the group or DM the voice message was sent in • Sender identity — your user ID is associated with each voice message d) How Voice Message Data Is Processed: • A short-lived audio file is created in your device's temporary directory • Upon sending, the file is uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage (path: voice_notes/{conversationId}/{userId}_{timestamp}.m4a) • Transmission occurs over an encrypted HTTPS connection • The temporary local file is deleted immediately after successful upload • Stored voice messages are retrieved via secure Firebase download URLs for playback e) Legal Basis (UK GDPR Article 6): • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): Voice message recording is based on your freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent — granted when you explicitly enable voice messages in Profile → Privacy & Security → Voice Messages. You may withdraw consent at any time. • Contract performance (Article 6(1)(b)): Transmission and storage of sent voice messages is necessary to deliver the messaging service you have contracted for. f) Your Consent Controls: • Voice messages are disabled by default — you must actively enable them in Profile → Privacy & Security → Voice Messages • Withdraw consent at any time by toggling the setting off — this prevents future recordings • Previously sent voice messages received by others are not automatically deleted; email privacy@huddl.app to request deletion of prior messages g) Data Retention: • Voice messages are retained for the life of the conversation, or until account deletion • All voice data in Firebase Storage is permanently deleted when you delete your Huddl account • Temporary local recording files are deleted immediately after upload or cancellation h) Data Sharing: • Voice messages are visible to all participants of the conversation they were sent in • Firebase Storage (Google LLC) processes audio files as our data processor under GDPR-compliant Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google's Privacy Policy • Voice messages are NOT shared with other third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models i) Children and Sensitive Data: Huddl is restricted to users aged 18+. Voice messages are never collected from minors. Audio recordings are not special category data under UK GDPR Article 9, but we apply equivalent care. j) Your Rights Regarding Voice Messages: • Access (Article 15): Request a list of voice messages associated with your account • Erasure (Article 17): Contact privacy@huddl.app to request deletion of specific voice messages • Withdraw consent (Article 7(3)): Toggle off in Profile → Privacy & Security at any time; withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing • Object (Article 21): Disable the feature and contact dpo@huddl.app k) Security Measures: • All audio data in transit encrypted with TLS 1.2+ • Firebase Storage ACLs restrict access to authenticated conversation participants only • Local temporary files stored in OS-sandboxed temporary directory, inaccessible to other apps l) Contact: Questions about voice message data processing: dpo@huddl.app
