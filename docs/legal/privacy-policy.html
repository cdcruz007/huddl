6. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, you have the following rights: a) Right of Access (Article 15): You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, including the purposes of processing, categories of data, and recipients. Exercise this right via Profile → Privacy & Security → View My Data, or by emailing privacy@huddl.app. b) Right to Rectification (Article 16): You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data. c) Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") (Article 17): You have the right to request deletion of your personal data where: • The data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected • You withdraw consent and no other legal basis applies • You object to processing and no overriding legitimate grounds exist • The data has been unlawfully processed d) Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18): You have the right to request that we restrict processing of your personal data in certain circumstances. e) Right to Data Portability (Article 20): You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and to transmit it to another controller. f) Right to Object (Article 21): You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes. g) Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making (Article 22): You have the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions that significantly affect you. h) Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7(3)): Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Exercising Your Rights: • In-app: Profile → Privacy & Security → View My Data / Delete Account • Email: privacy@huddl.app • Response time: Within 30 days of receipt of a valid request • Identity verification may be required Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113 if you believe your data protection rights have been violated.

16. Voice Messages & Microphone Access 🎙️

🎙️
Opt-in feature — explicit consent required (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a))
Voice messages are disabled by default. You must explicitly enable this feature in Profile → Privacy & Security → Voice Messages before you can record or send audio. You may withdraw consent at any time by toggling the feature off. See also: Terms of Service §18.
a) Overview: The voice message feature allows you to record and send short audio messages within group chats and direct messages on the huddl platform. This feature requires access to your device microphone and your explicit consent before use. b) Microphone Permission Scope: Microphone access is used exclusively for recording voice messages within the huddl app. It is not used for any background recording, passive listening, keyword detection, or any purpose other than the recording you actively initiate. The specific Android permission used is android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO. On iOS, the NSMicrophoneUsageDescription permission is used. c) Data Collected: When you use voice messages, we collect: • The audio recording itself (M4A/AAC format on mobile, WebM on web browsers) • Metadata: conversation ID, sender user ID, timestamp, file size • Temporary local file path (deleted immediately after upload) d) Processing Pipeline: 1. You tap Record — audio is captured by your device microphone 2. Audio is encoded locally (AAC-LC, 64 kbps, 16 kHz, mono) 3. On mobile: written to device temporary directory as a .m4a file 4. On web: created as a .webm blob URL in memory 5. On sending: uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage at path voice_notes/{conversationId}/{userId}_{timestamp}.{ext} 6. Local temporary file is immediately deleted after successful upload 7. Recipients receive a download URL to stream/play the message 8. No speech-to-text transcription is performed e) Legal Basis: • UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a) — Explicit Consent: Recording and uploading voice messages • UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b) — Contractual Necessity: Delivering voice messages to intended recipients within the Service f) Consent Controls: • Enable: Profile → Privacy & Security → Voice Messages (toggle on) → confirm consent dialog • Disable / Withdraw: Same screen (toggle off) → confirm withdrawal dialog • Default state: Disabled — no microphone access is requested until you actively enable the feature g) Retention and Deletion: • Voice messages are retained for the duration of the conversation or until your account is deleted • On account deletion, all voice messages stored under your user ID are permanently erased from Firebase Cloud Storage • Temporary local files are deleted immediately after upload and are never retained on device • To request early deletion of previously sent voice messages, contact privacy@huddl.app h) Data Sharing: Voice message audio files are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage (Google LLC). No other third parties receive access to your voice message data. Firebase is bound by a Data Processing Agreement and processes data in accordance with Google's privacy commitments. i) Children and Sensitive Data: Voice messages must not contain personal data of children (including names, schools, addresses, or identifying information). Do not send voice messages referencing or identifying specific children without appropriate consent. Voice messages are not classified as a special category of data under GDPR Article 9 unless you choose to include such information. j) Your Rights Regarding Voice Messages: • Access (Art. 15): Request a record of your voice message metadata via privacy@huddl.app • Erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion of previously sent voice messages via privacy@huddl.app • Withdrawal of Consent (Art. 7(3)): Disable the feature at any time in Profile → Privacy & Security → Voice Messages. Withdrawal prevents future recordings but does not automatically delete messages already delivered to other participants, as those form part of a shared conversation. Withdrawal is without prejudice to the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal • Objection (Art. 21): Contact privacy@huddl.app if you wish to object to any aspect of voice message data processing k) Security Measures: • All audio data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher • Audio files at rest in Firebase Storage are encrypted using AES-256 • Firebase Storage Access Control Lists (ACLs) restrict access to authorised parties only • Local temporary files are stored in the OS-sandboxed temporary directory and deleted immediately after upload • No audio content is retained on device after sending l) Data Protection Officer / Contact: For any queries specific to voice message data processing, contact: Email: privacy@huddl.app Subject line: "Voice Message Data — [your query]" Cruzen Ltd, 167-169 Great Portland Street, London W1W 5PF, UK
