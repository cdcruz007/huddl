Delete Your Account

You can delete your Huddl account and all associated data at any time. Choose the method that works best for you below.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be removed from our servers within 30 days.

Option 1 — Delete in the app

1
Open Huddl
Launch the app on your device
2
Go to Profile
Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar
3
Open Settings
Scroll down and tap Settings
4
Tap "Delete Account"
Found at the bottom of the Settings screen
5
Verify with OTP
Confirm your identity with the code sent to your phone number
6
Confirm deletion
Tap the final confirmation button — your account will be scheduled for deletion
or

Option 2 — Request by email

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email to request deletion:

Send your request to
privacy@huddapp.co.uk

Include your registered phone number in the email so we can identify your account. We will confirm deletion and process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Profile & account
Name, phone number, photo, postcode
Deleted
Messages & chats
Direct messages and group chat history
Deleted
Marketplace listings
All items you have listed for sale
Deleted
Group memberships
Removed from all groups and communities
Deleted
Events & RSVPs
Events you created or joined
Deleted
Voice messages
All recorded audio messages
Deleted
Subscription records
Billing records retained for legal compliance
Retained 7 yrs

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been permanently removed.
